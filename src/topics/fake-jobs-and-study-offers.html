01 · Explanation

Too goodto be true?

Fake job and study offer scams are a type of fraud in which criminals pretend to offer real employment opportunities, scholarships, internships or educational courses. Their goal is to steal people’s money, personal information or identity.

For example, a fake employer might offer a well-paid job without an interview and then ask the applicant to pay an upfront fee for training or equipment. Similarly, a fake university might offer a scholarship but require a payment to secure the place.

Be careful with personal information and never pay unexpected fees to secure a job or study opportunity.

02 · Story

Sarah and the€3,000 job.

Sarah, a 20-year-old student, found an online job offer promising €3,000 a month for working from home.

She applied and received a message the same day saying she had been hired without an interview. The recruiter asked her to pay €150 for training materials and send a copy of her passport.

Sarah felt suspicious, so she searched for the company online and discovered that its website was fake. She blocked the recruiter and reported the scam.
