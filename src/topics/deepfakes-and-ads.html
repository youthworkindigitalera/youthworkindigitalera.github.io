{{ICON:face}} What are deepfakes?

Deepfakes are fake videos, images or audio created using artificial intelligence (AI). They can make people look or sound like someone else.

Example: A fake video shows a famous person saying something they never said.

{{ICON:tag}} What are deceptive ads?

Deceptive ads are advertisements that use false information to trick people. They may try to steal money or personal information.

Example: An advert promises an expensive phone for only €5, but the website is a scam.

03 · Protect yourself

How can weprotect ourselves?

{{ICON:shield}} Five habits

  1. Check the source before believing or sharing a video.
  2. Look for strange movements or unnatural facial expressions.
  3. Avoid adverts that promise unrealistic discounts.
  4. Use strong passwords and two-factor authentication.
  5. Report suspicious videos and advertisements.
