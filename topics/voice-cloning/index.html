Voice cloning & family emergency scams

When a familiar voice becomes a scam

Stop. Verify. Call back.

01 · The story

“Mom, please help!I need money!”

One evening, Anna received a call from someone who sounded exactly like her son, Daniel.

Incoming call · “Daniel”

“Mom, please help! I’ve had an accident. I need money urgently!”

Terrified, Anna transferred her entire savings of €8,000 to the caller. Hours later, she called Daniel and discovered he had been safe at home all along. A scammer had used AI to clone his voice.

The money was gone, and Anna was devastated. She had to sell her twelve chickens to afford groceries, including her favorite chicken, Princess.

A week later, Anna sighed…“I lost my savings, my chickens, and my dream vacation… all because someone borrowed my son’s voice.”

02 · Explanation

When a voicecan be copied.

This story shows how AI voice cloning scams can have serious consequences. Voice cloning is a technology that uses artificial intelligence to copy a person’s voice and make it sound real.

Scammers use it to pretend to be someone’s family member and create panic by inventing emergencies. Victims may send money without checking if the story is true.

In Anna’s case, she lost all her savings because she trusted a voice she recognized. Even though selling her chickens may sound funny, it highlights how one fake phone call can turn someone’s life upside down.
