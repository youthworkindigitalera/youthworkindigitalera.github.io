Story

Ten minutesin the settings.

A fictional story written for this page.

Ola, 16, had a public Instagram, location on for every app, and a WhatsApp profile photo anyone could see.

One day a stranger messaged her: “Nice dog! Is that the park near your school?” He had never met her. Everything he knew came from her own posts and profile.

That evening Ola and her youth worker went through her settings together. Private account. Tags only from people she follows. Location “While using the app” instead of “Always”. Last seen and profile photo visible to “My contacts” only.

It took ten minutes. The stranger couldn’t see anything new, and Ola now does the same check every few months.

01 · Social & messaging platform hardening

Lock downyour socials.

Configuring platform privacy settings isolates your personal information from data scrapers, automated scam accounts, and open-source intelligence (OSINT) reconnaissance.

Instagram privacy

  • Account Privacy: Enable Private Account to block unauthorized followers and OSINT scrapers.
  • Tags & Mentions: Restrict tagging permissions to “People You Follow” or require manual approval.
  • Message Control: Restrict message requests to contacts only to avoid fraudulent links.
  • Activity Status: Disable “Show Activity Status” to protect online availability habits.

TikTok security

  • Account Type: Switch to Private Account.
  • Discoverability: Turn off “Suggest your account to others” (contacts and shared link recipients).
  • Content Interaction: Set Duet, Stitch, and Direct Messages to “Friends” or “No One”.
  • Downloads: Disable video downloads to prevent unauthorized reuse.

WhatsApp protection

  • Profile Metadata: Set Last Seen, Profile Photo, and About to “My Contacts” or “Nobody”.
  • Group Invitations: Change Groups to “My Contacts Except…” to avoid crypto/scam spam groups.
  • Silence Unknown Callers: Filter unsolicited calls and voice cloning attempts.
  • Protect IP Address: Enable Settings › Privacy › Advanced to relay calls via Meta servers.

02 · Operating system & device privileges

Your phoneknows a lot.

Mobile location protection

Set application location permissions to “While Using App” or “Never”. Disable Precise Location for social networks to prevent revealing exact physical addresses.

Media & photo privileges

Limit social media access to “Limited Access” (selected photos only). Strip location metadata (EXIF data) before sharing photos online.

Two lies,one fact.

Two lies, one fact: find the true statement about privacy settings.

Round 1 of 2Score 0

B is the fact. In WhatsApp you can choose who can add you to groups. A private account does not stop followers from sharing your posts, and apps with location permission can see your location without any tag.

A is the fact. You can limit location per app. Settings change when apps update, so check them every few months. Hiding your activity status only hides when you are online.
